# Safety And Limitations Standard

## Purpose

This standard defines the safety and limitation language required for future public model cards and release notes.

## Required Topics

- Intended use and users.
- Out-of-scope use.
- Known limitations.
- Bias and risk notes.
- Privacy and sensitive-data boundaries.
- Non-reliance notes for civic, educational, emergency, legal, medical, financial, or official decisions.
- Escalation or contact placeholder.
- Review status.

## Non-Claims

Safety language must not imply certification, legal compliance, emergency readiness, official service status, or complete risk elimination.
